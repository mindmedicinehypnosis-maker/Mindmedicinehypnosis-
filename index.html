Common questions about hypnosis

Will I lose control or say something embarrassing?

No. You stay aware and in control throughout, and you can open your eyes and stop at any time. Hypnosis is a focused, relaxed state, not sleep and not mind control.

Can I be hypnotized if I'm skeptical?

Yes. Skeptics are welcome. What matters most is that you genuinely want to change, not that you believe anything in advance.

Does it really work for quitting smoking?

Results vary from person to person, and no method works for everyone. Hypnosis tends to help most when you're ready to quit and want support with the habit and the triggers. I'll be honest with you on the free call about whether I think it's a good fit.

Do online sessions work as well as in person?

Many clients find video sessions comfortable because they're relaxing in their own space. All you need is a quiet room, a device with a camera, and an internet connection.
