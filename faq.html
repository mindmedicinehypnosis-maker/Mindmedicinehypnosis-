Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to common questions about hypnosis and working with me by video.

Does hypnosis work online?

Yes. Live video sessions work the same way as in-person ones: you relax, you stay awake, and I guide you with my voice. Many people find it easier to relax at home. Results vary from person to person, and I can't promise a specific outcome.

Will I be asleep or out of control?

No. You are awake and aware the whole time, and you stay in control. Nobody can make you do something you do not want to do.

What do I need for a video session?

A phone, tablet or computer with a camera and a reliable internet connection, and a quiet, private place where you can relax. Headphones can help.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on you and your goal. Many clients start with a three-session package. We will talk about what makes sense in our first conversation.

How much do sessions cost?

One session is $150, and a package of three sessions is $300. The first conversation is free.

What if I have already tried to quit and failed?

That is very common, and it does not mean you cannot quit. Many people try several times. We look at what got in the way and build a plan around it.

Can I use hypnosis along with nicotine patches or medication?

Many people do. Please talk with your doctor or pharmacist about patches, gum, lozenges or prescription options, especially if you take other medication or have a health condition.

Is hypnosis safe?

Hypnosis is generally considered safe for most people, but it is not right for everyone and it is not a substitute for medical or mental health care. Please tell me about any health conditions, and check with your doctor if you are unsure.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Nobody honestly can. I will always do my best to give you a clear plan and useful tools, and I will be honest with you along the way.

Where are you located, and who can you see?

I'm based in Pennsylvania and I work by live video with clients anywhere in the United States.

Is there something I can try for free first?

Yes. Take my free 2-minute quit-smoking quiz for a free starter guide, or my free anxiety quiz for a calming audio.
