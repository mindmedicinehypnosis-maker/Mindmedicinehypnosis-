Most people who smoke know the reasons to quit. What makes it hard is the habit: the morning coffee, the car, the stressful phone call, the break at work. Hypnosis is a way of working with those automatic patterns, using calm, focused attention, so that new choices feel more natural.

I'm Ron Dean, and I've been working with people on habits like this since 2003. My approach is simple: quitting does not have to be a fight.

How the sessions work

  1. We talk first. We go over your history with smoking, your triggers, and your reasons for wanting to quit.
  2. Guided relaxation. You relax in a comfortable spot at home, awake and in control, while I guide you with calm, positive suggestions that match your goal.
  3. Tools for between sessions. You leave with simple techniques for cravings and stress, and an audio to listen to at home.
